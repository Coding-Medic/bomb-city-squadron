Preserving American Military Aviation

The Bomb City Squadron of the Commemorative Air Force in Amarillo, Texas, is dedicated to preserving and honoring the history of American military aviation. Our members work to restore, maintain, and fly vintage aircraft.

Assigned Aircraft: 1943 Fairchild PT-19

1943 Fairchild PT-19 (Tail #78 / Reg: N519SH) outside the Commemorative Air Force hangar in Amarillo, TX.

The "Cradle of Heroes"

Finished in authentic pre-war U.S. Army Air Corps blue and yellow trainer colors, our 1943 Fairchild PT-19 (N519SH) represents the primary flight trainer that prepared thousands of Allied cadets for combat aircraft during World War II.

Registration N519SH (Nose #78)
Aircraft 1943 Fairchild PT-19 Cornell
Powerplant Ranger L-440 6-Cylinder Inline (175 HP)
Configuration Tandem Open-Cockpit Low-Wing Monoplane
Home Hangar Tradewind Airport (KTDW), Amarillo, TX

Squadron Events & Programs

Annual Fundraiser • Tradewind Airport

Annual Hangar Dance

Step back into the 1940s at Tradewind Airport! Featuring live big band swing music from Patrick Swindell & Esquire Jazz, vintage dinner, 1940s photo booth, period attire, and our PT-19 on display.

Living History Flight Experience

Take a Ride in a PT-19!

Experience open-cockpit World War II flight firsthand over the Texas Panhandle. Watch our Facebook page for upcoming PT-19 ride raffle drawings ($10 tickets) and fly-in ride days.

Regional Fly-Ins & Community

Bomb City Squadron regularly participates in local fly-ins, veterans tributes, and community events like the Props & Pistons Car Show & Fly-In at Tradewind Airport.
